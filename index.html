Common Questions

Questions we get
before people drive over

Can I buy LUXE paint protection film locally in Southern California?

Yes. Select LUXE films are held at the Santa Clarita / Valencia hub for local purchase, pickup, and installation, rather than shipped in for each job. Availability changes by product line, finish, width, and quantity, so the hub confirms what is on hand before pickup, ordering, or installation. Request current availability.

Do you install the film, or only sell it?

Both. The facility has a dedicated, fully lit installation bay, and patterns are cut in-house on a Graphtec FC9000 a few steps from where they go on the car. You can also buy film to take away. Request an installation quote.

Where is the Southern California hub, and can I come and look first?

27820 Fremont Ct, Ste 1, Santa Clarita (Valencia), CA 91355. Yes — that is largely what the showroom is for. Color and finish rarely photograph honestly, so the display space lets you see a film under real light, against real paint, and decide with your own eyes. Request a showroom visit.

Which LUXE products can I see or order through the hub?

The LUXE range covered here includes Gloss Is Boss, Stealth Matte, Black Series, Windshield Guard, the Color Series, and Glide InstaGel installation gel. Products shown on this site may not all be physically stocked at the hub at any given moment — ask before you plan a job around one.

How many LUXE colors are there?

The full LUXE TPU Color PPF deck runs to 175 finishes across Gloss, Metallic, Satin & Matte, Liquid Metal, Specialty and Glow. The Color Series line itself comprises 40 finishes. Locally stocked colors vary, and display samples are not a guarantee of stock. Browse all 175 finishes.

I run a shop. Can I buy LUXE film here rather than waiting on a shipment?

That is one of the reasons the hub exists. It is a local point of contact for approved installers, dealers, product enquiries, availability requests, and supply. Installers working locally can collect stocked items the same day instead of waiting on a delivery. Trade enquiries are handled on a separate path from retail ones, so they reach the right person first time. Contact the hub about supply.

Is Gravity Garage part of LUXE Protection Films?

No. They are two separate companies. Gravity Garage is an independently owned and operated automotive business acting as LUXE's Southern California regional stocking, showroom, sales, and installation hub, and it manages the day-to-day running of the facility and its installation work. LUXE Protection Films remains the brand owner and controls the LUXE product program, this website, its forms, analytics, and customer-data systems. Nothing on this site creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise, or shared ownership between the two.

Is the installation covered by a warranty?

Coverage is available on approved installations. Warranty terms are administered by LUXE Protection Films, not by the hub — so ask the hub what applies to your film and your vehicle, and the terms come from LUXE. Terms & warranty information↗
